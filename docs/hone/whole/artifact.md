# Riak: Cozy Aquarium - review packet

Web app (PWA, offline, free, English + Bahasa Indonesia, light/dark). Screenshots attached: tank (desktop
and phone), care sheet, fishing scene, fishing fight, garden, journal, focus, koi pond.

Screens and interactions:
- Tank: animated vector aquarium; fish school, sleep at night, wake for food; tap a fish for its card
  (name, personality, mood, size, rename, sell, release); tap glass to attract fish. Tools row: Feed, Care,
  Scape, Residents, Shop, Photo, Watch (full-screen clock, keeps screen awake), Share (read-only visit link).
  Side column: water status, water test readings, today's care checklist with streak, daily orders.
- Care: nitrogen cycle simulation (ammonia, nitrite, nitrate), water test, water change, glass wipe,
  heater and light schedule, plant trimming into cuttings.
- Fishing: 9 spots with weather and day/night. Flick up on the water to cast (swipe speed and straightness set
  the distance), flick again to strike, crank an on-screen reel and play line tension against fish strength with
  drag settings; classic hold controls kept. 5 baits, 5 lures, line upgrades, tournaments against CPU anglers
  with hot-seat players, 12 quests, photo album with trophy cards, challenge links. 47 species, 5 rarities.
- Breeding with inherited colours; aquascaping with 16 plants, 8 hardscape pieces, 3 substrates, 3 tank
  sizes, second tank and reef room.
- Greenhouse trays (cuttings, harvest, variegated sports, houseplants to sell); reef lab (frag, cross,
  named strains from form, two colours, pattern).
- Journal: fish dex with silhouettes and hints, plants, strains, 18 milestones. Focus timer 15-60 min with
  weekly bars and box breathing. Offline catch-up summary on return.

Look (v1.4): warm cream page with a soft top glow, floating white cards with layered shadows, pill buttons
and a floating nav; the tank is drawn as a glass box (perspective side panes, receding gravel floor, depth
haze, back-glass planting silhouettes, fish floor shadows, lid rim, stand). Stones and driftwood carry
contact shadows, a top-light highlight, a core shadow and a moss cap; fish are drawn larger, with small
species lifted most. Each of the 47 species has its own body form and fins used in the tank, cards and
journal silhouettes. Each fishing spot has its own scenery and a drawn rod; water readings are tiles.
Greenhouse trays are illustrated bays of a glasshouse: glazing bars over a warm sky, garden shrubs
outside, a timber bench and a cell seed tray whose side cells sprout as the main plant grows; empty bays
show a seed packet and trowel. Side cards read like a field guide (numbered plates I and II, "No. 1"
order entries, dotted ledger lines) and end in arrow links to the next step of each loop (harvest a tray,
trim plants in the tank, plant cuttings with Scape, fish orders on the Tank screen). Focus puts box
breathing beside the timer and shows an empty week as dashed 25 minute targets with a short note.


Koi pond (v1.5): a separate Pond tab, a top-down koi pond to relax with. Nine koi varieties swim with a spine-follow motion among lily pads and lotus, under swaying canopy shade, with drifting maple and ginkgo leaves, rim stones, lanterns and a bamboo fountain. Tap for ripples, hold to feed, drag to lead the school, tap a koi for its name card. A generative sound mix (stream, fountain, chimes, crickets, rain) with a level per layer, day / dusk / night moods, rain, a sleep timer and a full-screen calm mode. Controls are one quiet row that fades after 10 s idle.

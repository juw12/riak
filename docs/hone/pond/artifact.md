# Riak koi pond - review packet

Pond tab in Riak (free offline web app, English + Bahasa Indonesia). Screenshots: day pond (1440), koi
feeding (1440), night pond (1440), day pond on a phone (390), the Sounds sheet (390), calm full-screen mode at
dusk (390).

What it does:
- Top-down koi pond drawn live on canvas: 9 koi varieties (kohaku, sanke, showa, ogon, asagi, tancho,
  chagoi, yamabuki, kujaku), each with a name; bodies bend as they swim; fins, tail, patterns, shadows.
- Scene: stone rim with moss and grass, two stone lanterns, bamboo shishi-odoshi fountain that tips and
  knocks, lily pads with lotus, drifting maple, ginkgo and blossom leaves, caustic light, ripples.
- Moods: Auto follows the clock; Day, Dusk, Night (lantern glow, moon reflection, fireflies). Rain toggle
  adds rain rings and rain sound.
- Interactions: tap water for a ripple (koi come to look), hold to feed, drag to lead koi, tap a koi for its
  name card, tap a leaf to spin it; keys Space, F, R, M, H, Esc.
- Sound (generated live in the browser, no audio files): stream, fountain trickle and wooden knock, wind
  chimes, night crickets, rain, plops on taps, pellet ticks, gulps when koi eat; sounds are panned by
  position. Sounds sheet: volume, layer toggles, mood, koi count 5/9/13, sleep timer 10/20/30 min that fades
  out and dims the pond. Eye button: calm full-screen mode with all UI hidden.
- Stats line: minutes by the pond, pellets eaten, koi met.

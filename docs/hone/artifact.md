# Riak: Cozy Aquarium - review packet

Web app (PWA, offline, free, English + Bahasa Indonesia, light/dark). Screenshots attached: tank (desktop
and phone), care sheet, fishing scene, reel minigame, garden, reef lab, journal, focus.

Screens and interactions:
- Tank: animated vector aquarium; fish school, sleep at night, wake for food; tap a fish for its card
  (name, personality, mood, size, rename, sell, release); tap glass to attract fish. Tools row: Feed, Care,
  Scape, Residents, Shop, Photo, Watch (full-screen clock, keeps screen awake), Share (read-only visit link).
  Side column: water status, water test readings, today's care checklist with streak, daily orders.
- Care: nitrogen cycle simulation (ammonia, nitrite, nitrate), water test, water change, glass wipe,
  heater and light schedule, plant trimming into cuttings.
- Fishing: 9 spots with weather and day/night, bait choice with live bite odds, hold-to-cast, nibble then
  bite window, reel tension minigame, keep / bucket / sell / release. 47 species, 5 rarities, golden variants.
- Breeding with inherited colours; aquascaping with 16 plants, 8 hardscape pieces, 3 substrates, 3 tank
  sizes, second tank and reef room.
- Greenhouse trays (cuttings, harvest, variegated sports, houseplants to sell); reef lab (frag, cross,
  named strains from form, two colours, pattern).
- Journal: fish dex with silhouettes and hints, plants, strains, 18 milestones. Focus timer 15-60 min with
  weekly bars and box breathing. Offline catch-up summary on return.
